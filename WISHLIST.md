# Wish List

1. **Tower Upgrades** -- Spend scrap to level up placed towers (3 tiers). Each
   tier boosts damage/range/fire rate and changes the sprite color intensity.
   Click a placed tower to see upgrade options.

2. **Sell Towers** -- Middle-click or hotkey on a placed tower to sell it back
   for 60% of its cost. Opens up maze redesign mid-game without feeling
   punished.

3. **Boss Waves** -- Every 5th wave spawns a boss: massive HP, larger sprite,
   drops a scrap pile worth 5x normal loot. Bosses could have unique traits
   (regeneration, armor, splitting into smaller enemies on death).

4. **Camera Zoom & Pan** -- Scroll wheel to zoom in/out, middle-drag to pan.
   Essential once maps get bigger or you want to admire your maze.

5. **Chain Lightning Tower** -- New tower type that arcs damage between nearby
   enemies. Each arc does 70% of the previous hit's damage. Devastating against
   clustered groups in tight maze corridors.

6. **Terrain Types** -- Some grid cells are rubble (impassable, free walls),
   puddles (enemies slow down), or radioactive (enemies take damage over time).
   Makes each map feel unique and adds strategic depth.

7. **Endless Mode** -- After wave 20, keep generating increasingly absurd waves
   forever. Track high score (waves survived). Leaderboard someday?

8. **Sound & Juice** -- Scrap pickup clink, tower firing sounds, explosion
   rumble, enemy death squelch. Screen shake on explosive hits. Particle
   effects for projectile impacts and scrap drops.

9. **Multiple Spawn/Goal Points** -- Maps with 2-3 spawn points or goals.
   Forces the player to split their defenses and creates more interesting
   mazing puzzles. Enemies could take different routes each wave.

10. **Scrap Magnet** -- Passive upgrade (bought between waves) that
    auto-collects scrap within a radius. Tiers increase the radius. Frees the
    player from right-click duty so they can focus on tower placement during
    intense waves.

11. **Smart Targeting** -- Let the player cycle targeting modes per tower (e.g.
    lowest HP, closest, furthest along path, highest HP). Currently all towers
    default to lowest HP. Could be a click-to-toggle on placed towers, or an
    upgrade unlock that adds new modes.

12. **Full CI / CD Pipeline** -- GitHub Actions workflow that builds and tests on
    Linux, Windows, and macOS. On tagged releases, cross-compile release binaries
    for all three platforms and publish to itch.io automatically via
    butler.
